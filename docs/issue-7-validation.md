## Limits and stop

This is an integration proof on one approved family, not new calibration or a real-context safety guarantee. Six manually designed synthetic families share design assumptions; needed-fragment necessity and general error bounds remain unestablished. Byte savings do not establish token, money or latency savings.

Native tool folders provide operational workdir separation; the session cwd remains shared and full originals remain accessible. The native bridge used sequential trusted primary-agent dispatch after the consumer's synchronous callback check. Offline tests prove revoke/revision rejection at callback invocation; this is not an OS-wide atomicity or sandbox claim.

An initial restricted launcher attempt stopped before any owner/HTTP/native operation. The permitted ordinary-account execution then used one live server instance; no active budget was restarted or replaced. Global model/hooks/native memory/compaction/settings were not edited. Opt-in was revoked before disabled dispatch and again at stop. No treatment remains active.
